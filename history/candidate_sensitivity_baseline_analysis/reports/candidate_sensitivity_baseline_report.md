# Candidate threshold sensitivity and temporal baselines

The reference multicriterion rule selected 462 material-transition events and 406 passed the next-year rule (87.9%). Same-size percentile-only and raw-tail baselines reached 87.9% and 87.9%, respectively. The weighted random same-size expectation was 72.3%. Exact transition-wise hypergeometric enrichment tests are reported without treating transition events as independent biological replicates.

The threshold surface contains 768 fixed combinations. Combined with three pedigree cut heights, the full-funnel multiverse contains 2304 complete decision paths that propagate robustness thresholds, evidence-duration gates, and pedigree de-duplication to final portfolio membership. It is used to show choice sensitivity, not to choose a post hoc optimal threshold.

The breeding application table integrates evidence duration, disease tail, LOCAL CHECK contrast, temporal outcomes, background-adjusted rank, observed failure frequency, and location-by-sowing risk for all 14 prospective candidates.
